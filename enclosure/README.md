The board details from the original note: 90 × 64 mm, 4 × M3 plated holes 4 mm in from each corner. There are 8 × JST PA-3 sensor plugs along the bottom edge, 2 × PA-4 for power/CAN, a vertical USB-C, and BOOT/RESET buttons. Keep the lid clear of metal over the ESP32-S3 antenna (top edge, x 63–82 mm).
